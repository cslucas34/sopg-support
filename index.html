System Operator Pocket Guide

App support

For help with a practice exam, calculator, or installation, email SOPG support. Include the app version, your iPhone or iPad model, and the steps leading up to the issue. For a question correction, include the topic and the first few words of the question.

Privacy

SOPG requires no account. The developer does not collect data through the app. Your study preferences are stored on your device. Links to official study materials open external websites, which have their own privacy policies.

If you contact support, your email address and the details you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please omit employer records and confidential operating information.
